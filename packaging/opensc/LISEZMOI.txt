  macOS  : /Library/OpenSC/lib/opensc-pkcs11.so ou /usr/local/lib/opensc-pkcs11.so (installeur OpenSC)
  Debian : /usr/lib/x86_64-linux-gnu/opensc-pkcs11.so (paquet opensc-pkcs11)
